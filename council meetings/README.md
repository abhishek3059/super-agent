## Purpose and provenance

These records reorganise the signed design's source council deliberation for readable handoff.

They are a readable reorganization of the source, not new meetings, fresh independent reviews, or verbatim transcripts.

- Source: [docs/delivery/council.md](../docs/delivery/council.md), dated 2026-11-07.
- Mode: quick mode with four independent `general` subagent sessions, followed by anonymized cross-review and chairman synthesis.
- Caveat: perspective diversity does not prove different model families.
- Specialised architect/reviewer/security profiles were available for subsequent design gates.
